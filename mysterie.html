MYSTERIES AND CONSTRUCTION

From Djoser's experimental steps to the smooth, star-aligned faces of Giza — the evolution of the pyramid form, told through six monuments.

CONSTRUCTION THEORIES

HOW WERE THE PYRAMIDS BUILT?

TRANSPORT

STONE TRANSPORTATION

Limestone blocks came from nearby quarries; granite traveled hundreds of kilometers by barge from Aswan during the Nile's annual flood.

METHOD

RAMPS & LEVERS

Straight, spiral, and zig-zag ramp designs have all been proposed. Wooden sledges dragged over wetted sand helped reduce friction.

LABOR

THE WORKERS

Evidence from ancient Egypt describes organized, skilled crews of workers who were fed and housed near the construction sites.

TOOLS

COPPER & STONE TOOLS

Without iron, workers used copper chisels, dolerite pounding stones, wooden levers, ropes, and other simple tools.

OPEN QUESTIONS

THE MYSTERIES THAT REMAIN

HOW WERE THEY ALIGNED SO PRECISELY?

The pyramids were carefully aligned with the cardinal directions, using observations of the sun and stars.

WHY WERE THEY BUILT AT ALL?

Pyramids served as royal tombs and were connected to ancient Egyptian beliefs about resurrection and the journey of the king's spirit into the afterlife.

WHAT WAS ORIGINALLY INSIDE THEM?

Many pyramids contained burial chambers, passages, sarcophagi, and objects connected to the king's journey into the afterlife.

HOW HAVE THEY SURVIVED SO LONG?

Their massive stone construction, carefully chosen locations, and relatively dry Egyptian climate helped preserve them for thousands of years.

ARE THERE HIDDEN CHAMBERS LEFT TO FIND?

Modern scanning technologies have revealed previously unknown spaces inside some pyramids, suggesting that more discoveries may still be possible.
