LIFE ALONG THE NILE

How Did Ancient Egyptians Live?

Life in Ancient Egypt was closely connected to the Nile River. The river provided water, fertile land, transportation and opportunities for trade.

DAILY LIFE

Families lived in homes made from mud bricks and communities developed around the Nile.

FOOD & FARMING

Farmers grew wheat, barley, vegetables and other crops using the fertile land near the Nile.
