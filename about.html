ANCIENT EGYPT

THE LAND OF PHARAOHS

A civilization shaped by the Nile, powerful rulers, magnificent monuments, and thousands of years of history.

A CIVILIZATION

THAT ENDURED

Ancient Egypt developed along the banks of the Nile River and became one of history's most remarkable civilizations.

Its achievements in architecture, writing, art and engineering continue to fascinate people around the world.

THE NILE RIVER

THE GIFT OF EGYPT

The Nile was central to life in Ancient Egypt. Its waters supported agriculture, transportation, trade and settlements.
