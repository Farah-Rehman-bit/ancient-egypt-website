SIX STRUCTURE, SIX CENTURIES

THE PYRAMIDS

From Djoser,s experimental steps to the smooth, star-aligned faces of Giza — the evolution of the pyramid form. told through six monuments.

GIZA PLATEAU • C. 2670 BCE
STEP PYRAMID OF DJOSER

The world's first monumenteal stone building — six mastabas stacked by the architect IMhotep, the pyramid's true origin.

DAHSHUR • C. 2600 BCE
BENT PYRAMID

Its angle changes mid-build, from 54° to 43° — physical evidence of Egypt's engineers correcting a structural mistake in real time.

DAHSHUR • C. 2590 BCE
RED PYRAMID

Named for the rust-tond limestone beneath its casing, this is the first successful true (smooth-sided) pyramid ever completed.

GIZA PLATEAU • C. 2570 BCE
PYRAMID OF KHAFRE

Appears taller than Khufu's due to higher ground and a steeper angle — still wears a cap of original casing stone at its peak.

GIZA PLATEAU • C. 2560 BCE
GREAT PYRAMID OF GIZA

Built for Khufu, the tallest structure on Earth for almost 3,800 years — amd still the largest pyramid by volume.

GIZA PLATEAU • C. 2510 BCE
PYRAMID OF MENKAURE

The smallest of the Giza three, faced party in red granite — later damage by an unfinished attempt to dismantle it.
