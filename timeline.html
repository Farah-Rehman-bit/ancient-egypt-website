THE JOURNEY BEGINS

A CIVILIZATION THROUGH TIME

Ancient Egypt developed along the Nile River and became one of the world's most remarkable civilizations. Explore the major periods that shaped Egyptian history.

c. 6000–3100 BCE
BEGINNING

PREHISTORIC EGYPT

Early communities settled along the Nile, developing agriculture, villages, trade, and early forms of Egyptian culture.

c. 3100–2686 BCE
UNIFICATION

EARLY DYNASTIC PERIOD

Upper and Lower Egypt were united under a single ruler. The foundations of the Egyptian state and royal traditions were established.

c. 2686–2181 BCE
AGE OF PYRAMIDS

OLD KINGDOM

Known as the Age of the Pyramids, this period saw the construction of monumental structures including the Step Pyramid and the pyramids of Giza.

c. 2055–1650 BCE
REUNIFICATION

MIDDLE KINGDOM

Egypt was reunified after a period of division. Art, literature, architecture, and trade flourished during this important era.

c. 1550–1070 BCE
GOLDEN AGE

NEW KINGDOM

Egypt reached the height of its power. Great rulers, temples, monuments, and royal tombs transformed the civilization.

c. 664–332 BCE
LAST NATIVE DYNASTIES

LATE PERIOD

Egypt experienced periods of foreign rule while continuing to preserve its ancient traditions, religion, art, and culture.

332–30 BCE
GREEK INFLUENCE

PTOLEMAIC PERIOD

After Alexander the Great's conquest, Egypt was ruled by the Ptolemaic dynasty. Greek and Egyptian traditions existed side by side.

30 BCE – 641 CE
ROMAN RULE

ROMAN EGYPT

Egypt became part of the Roman Empire. Alexandria remained an important center of trade, learning, and culture.
