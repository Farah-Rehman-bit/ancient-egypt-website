THE PHARAOHS

Every Egyptian king's name was enclosed in a cartouche — a protective loop of rope meant to guard it for eternity. Four of those names built the pyramids you just walked through.

Khufu · Khafre · Menkaure · Djoser

Menkaure (Greek: Mycernius) built the smallest of the three Giza pyramids but finished it with unsually fine granite work. Later Greeek historians described him as a just and gentle ruler compared with his predecessors.

DJOSER

Djoser was the first king of the 3rd Dynasty to commission a monumental stone tomb. Working with his official Imhotep, he set the template every later pyramid would follow and refine.

Dynasty3rd
